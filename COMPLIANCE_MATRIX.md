# IronMesh Compliance Matrix

Alignment of IronMesh controls with ETSI EN 303 645 v2.1.1 (Cyber Security for Consumer Internet of
Things: Baseline Requirements), NIST IR 8259A (IoT Device Cybersecurity Capability Core Baseline),
IEC 62443-4-2 (component requirements) and the essential requirements of the EU Cyber Resilience Act
(Regulation (EU) 2024/2847, Annex I).

## How to read this matrix

| Column        | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                 |
|---------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Clause        | The provision number as printed in the standard. Requirement text is paraphrased; consult the standard for normative wording. ETSI "shall" provisions are marked (M), "should" provisions (R), conditional provisions (C). |
| IronMesh control | What the platform, firmware or process does. Terms follow `docs/DESIGN_CONTRACT.md` and `docs/API_CONTRACT.md`.                                                      |
| Evidence      | A test, file or document that demonstrates the control. Paths are repository-relative; `path:line (name)` points at a test or symbol in that file and is kept current by `scripts/resolve_threat_refs.py`. |
| Status        | `Satisfied`, `Partially` (control exists, a stated gap remains), `Not applicable` (with reason), `Not satisfied`, or `Integrator responsibility` (the provision concerns the physical product or the end-user relationship that the device maker or tenant controls; guidance is given). |

Scope note. IronMesh is a framework and platform: it ships firmware components (`device/esp32`,
`device/agent-rs`), cloud services and operating procedures, and is integrated into a product by a
device maker ("integrator") and operated for end users by a tenant. Provisions about enclosures,
labels, consumer notices and consent cannot be satisfied by IronMesh alone and are marked
accordingly rather than claimed. Every evidence path and symbol exists in the repository and is
checked in CI by `scripts/resolve_threat_refs.py --strict`; where the implementation falls short of the
stated control, the status is `Partially` and the gap is listed in section 4. Vulnerability handling
and support-period provisions are satisfied by `SECURITY.md` and the RFC 9116 file
`docs/security.txt`.
